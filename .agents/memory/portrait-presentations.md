---
name: Portrait presentation exports
description: Why portrait presentation handouts retain a landscape editor frame.
---

**Rule:** Keep the slides platform's fixed landscape wrappers, routes and export contract intact for portrait handouts. Compose centered portrait artwork, export normally, then extract and resize that artwork into physical A4 PDF pages.

**Why:** The platform requires landscape wrappers for slide identification and export. Changing those wrappers to portrait can break exports even when the preview looks correct.

**How to apply:** Treat the landscape editor gutters as non-deliverable space. Verify the final PDF's portrait page dimensions, page count and content separately. A standard viewer export is not automatically the A4 handout.
