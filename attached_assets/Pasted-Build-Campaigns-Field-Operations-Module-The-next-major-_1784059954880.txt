Build Campaigns & Field Operations Module

The next major module in JanSetu should be Campaigns & Field Operations.

This should become one of the core modules of JanSetu and replace the need to build separate modules for Membership Drives, Surveys, Government Scheme Registration, Health Camps, Booth Strengthening, etc.

The module must be generic, scalable, mobile-first, and integrate deeply with the existing JanSetu ecosystem.

Objective

Allow representatives and office staff to:

Create campaigns
Assign workers
Build custom forms
Collect data
Manage field activities
Track progress
Generate reports
Analyze results

Everything from one unified module.

Response Modes

Every campaign must support one of three response modes.

1. Linked to Citizen

Worker searches an existing citizen.

If found:

Attach the response to Citizen 360° Profile.

If not found:

Allow creating a new citizen.

Used for:

Membership Drive
Government Scheme Registration
First-Time Voter Registration
Complaint Follow-up
Health Camp
2. Anonymous

No citizen information is stored.

Used for:

Opinion Poll
Exit Poll
Satisfaction Survey
Feedback Survey

Only aggregate reports should be generated.

3. Optional

Worker decides whether to link the response to a citizen or keep it anonymous.

Important

Do not build this as a simple survey module.

Build it as a complete Campaigns & Field Operations platform that combines campaign management, surveys, data collection, worker assignments, task management, offline field work, analytics, and reporting into one integrated system.

It should become one of the flagship capabilities of JanSetu and be usable year-round for governance, citizen outreach, and organizational activities—not just during elections.
